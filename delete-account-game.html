Delete your game account

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your account for Football League - DOFA, the mobile game published by Delta Allied Sports, and the data held with it.

Deleting is permanent. Your display name, scores and game progress are erased and cannot be restored, on this or any device. Deleting your account does not refund purchases — see purchases and refunds below.

Ask us to delete your account

Send us a request and we will delete the account and its data. You do not need to reinstall the game.

By request

Send us a deletion request

Email Mounir.imam@deltagroup-me.com with the subject Football League - DOFA account deletion request, or use our contact form.

So we can find the right account and be sure the request is really yours, please include:

  • The email address the game account uses
  • The display name shown on leaderboards
  • If you are a parent or guardian asking on behalf of a child, say so and give the display name

We may ask one follow-up question to confirm the account is yours. We will never ask for your password.

What gets deleted

WhatDetail
Your account and sign-inThe account itself and the email address used to sign in and recover it. You will not be able to sign in again.
Your display nameRemoved from the game and from every leaderboard it appears on.
Your scores and progressErased, including leaderboard entries and any saved progress.

The game contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs, so there is no advertising profile or tracking history to delete — none was ever collected.

What is kept, and why

One thing is kept: a record of purchases you made.

Tax and accounting rules require a business to keep records of its transactions for a set period, so we cannot delete a purchase record on request. It is kept against the transaction rather than against your name, and it is deleted once that period expires. Apple and Google also hold their own records of the same purchases, independently of us.

We never receive or store your card details. Payment is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play.

Purchases and refunds

Deleting your game account does not refund anything you have bought, and we cannot issue refunds ourselves — purchases are made with Apple or Google, not with us. Refunds are requested through the App Store or Google Play.

If you want a refund, ask for it before deleting your account. Once the account is gone we cannot look up what it bought, which makes supporting a refund claim harder. If you bought anything on a recurring basis, cancel it in the store as well — deleting your game account does not cancel a store subscription.

How long it takes

We action deletion requests within 30 days, and usually much sooner. You will get an email confirming when it is done. Backups are overwritten on our provider's normal cycle.
