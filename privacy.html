1. Who we are

Delta Allied Sports is a sports management and consulting company established in 2019 and headquartered in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. We manage athlete careers, operate youth football and basketball tournaments across the GCC, produce sports media, and develop sports-related mobile games.

Questions about this policy, or any request about your data, should go to info@deltagroup-me.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to:

  • Our website at deltagroup-me.com, including the contact form and the tournament registration page
  • Our tournament app, used to run competitions, accreditation and matchday operations
  • Our mobile games, including Football League - DOFA

It does not cover other organisations' websites or services that we link to, such as our social media pages. Those have their own privacy policies.

3. The website

Browsing the site

We want to be direct about this, because it is unusual: our website contains no analytics, no advertising trackers and no third-party tracking cookies. We do not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, session recording, or any comparable tool. We do not build profiles of visitors, and we do not track you across other websites.

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, which include IP addresses, for security and reliability purposes.

Loading the site also requests fonts from Google Fonts and Fontshare. As with any resource loaded from another provider, this means your IP address is visible to them. We use these services purely to display the site's typefaces.

The contact form

When you send us a message we collect your first name, last name, email address, subject and message. We use this only to reply to you and to keep a record of the enquiry. The form is delivered through Formspree, and the resulting email is received in our Google Workspace inbox.

5. The tournament app

Our tournament app is used by organisers, teams, coaches, officials and players to run competitions. It is a more comprehensive system than the website, and handles more sensitive information. It holds:

  • People records — full name, date of birth, phone, email and a profile photograph, along with the person's role (player, coach, official, staff)
  • Team and squad records — team membership, jersey numbers, and staff assignments to tournaments and venues
  • Digital ID cards — an accreditation credential issued for a tournament, with an associated QR token, status and expiry
  • Accreditation scans — when a digital ID is scanned at a match, we record which credential was scanned, at which match, by whom, when, and whether it was accepted. These records show attendance at specific matches and venues.
  • Match records — fixtures, results and match events such as goals and cards
  • Notifications — a record of match-related notifications sent to a person
  • Push notification tokens — a device token and platform, used only to deliver notifications to your device

We use this information solely to organise and deliver competitions: registering teams, issuing accreditation, verifying who may enter a venue or take part in a match, recording results, and sending fixture information.

Accreditation scan records deserve particular attention because they show where and when a person was present. We use them only to verify eligibility and attendance at the event itself. We do not use them to track people outside tournament venues and matchdays, and we do not use them for any purpose unrelated to running the competition.

6. Deleting your tournament app account

You can delete your tournament app account, and the personal data held with it, at any time — either from inside the app itself or by asking us. Instructions for both, including what to put in a request, are on our account deletion page.

Deletion is permanent. We cannot restore an account or its details afterwards.

What we keep, and why

Records of matches that have already been played are not erased. Goals, cards and other match events, fixtures you refereed or confirmed the result of, and historical squad entries all stay in the competition's records — but the link to your identity is removed, and your name is replaced with a neutral label, so what remains does not identify you.

We keep these because a completed competition's results have to stay accurate for everyone who took part. Removing one participant's events would change the standings of a tournament that is already finished, affecting other teams and players. This retention is deliberately narrow: it covers the competition record itself and nothing else. Your contact details, photograph, accreditation and device information are all deleted.

If your account organises a tournament

Every tournament must have an organizer, so an account that currently runs one cannot be deleted until someone else takes it over. We will tell you exactly which tournaments need transferring, and the deletion continues once you have named the person taking over. We do not reassign a tournament to someone without being asked to, and we do not delete a tournament in order to delete an account.

7. Our mobile games

Football League - DOFA is a mobile football runner game. We want to be clear about what it does not do: it contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs. Many mobile games, especially free ones, are built around advertising identifiers. Ours is not.

The game offers player accounts and in-app purchases. You sign in with your Apple or Google account rather than creating a password with us, so we receive only the basic account information that sign-in passes to us. Alongside it we hold your in-app activity — game progress, team data and usage — and a record of purchases you have made. The game does not ask for your postal address or phone number.

The game is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through it, and features involving purchases or links out of the game sit behind a parental gate. This is different from the tournament side of our business, described in section 8, where we deliberately do hold data about young players in order to run competitions.

You can delete your game account and the data held with it at any time. Our game account deletion page explains how, what is removed, and what happens to purchases.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple and Google through their app stores. We never receive or store your payment card details.

8. Children's privacy

Much of what we do involves children. We run youth football and basketball competitions, so a significant amount of the personal data we hold relates to players under 18. We treat that data with corresponding care.

Consent

A player under 18 must be registered by their parent or legal guardian, or by an academy or club acting with the guardian's authority. When you register a young player you confirm that you are their parent or guardian, or that you are authorised to act for them, and you consent to us processing their details as described in this policy. We record that consent together with the registration.

What we collect about children, and what we do not

For a young player we collect the information listed in sections 4 and 5 — name, date of birth, nationality, club, position, contact details, and where the tournament app is used, a profile photograph and accreditation records.

We do not use children's data for marketing. We do not sell it. We do not use it to build advertising profiles, and our games contain no advertising SDKs.

Photographs and accreditation

Where the tournament app is used, a player's photograph appears on their digital ID so that officials can confirm the right person is taking part. This is a safeguarding measure. The photograph is used for accreditation and is not published.

Media at events

We also film and photograph matches and events as part of our media and sponsorship work. Where images of young players may be published or broadcast, we handle consent for that separately from tournament registration. Consent is asked for from the parent or guardian in writing before the event, on a separate media consent form that names the event and explains where images may be used. It is optional: a player may take part in a competition without agreeing to it, and we will not treat a refusal as a reason to exclude anyone. You may withdraw consent at any time by emailing info@deltagroup-me.com, and we will stop using those images in anything we publish from that point onward. Material that has already been printed or broadcast cannot always be recalled, and we will tell you honestly when that is the case.

Guardian rights

A parent or guardian may at any time ask to see what we hold about their child, have it corrected, have it deleted, or withdraw consent. Contact us at info@deltagroup-me.com and we will act on the request. Withdrawing consent may mean the player can no longer take part in a competition, because we need this information to run it safely.

13. How we protect it

Some specifics, rather than vague reassurance:

  • Registration data is not publicly readable. The public key used by our website has no ability to read, change or delete registrations — it cannot even list them. This is enforced both by database permissions and by row-level security, independently of one another.
  • Registrations are written only through a controlled server-side endpoint that validates every submission. The website never writes to the database directly.
  • IP addresses used for rate limiting are irreversibly hashed, never stored in the clear.
  • All traffic to our website and services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.
  • Access to registration data is limited to staff who need it to run the competition.

No system is perfectly secure, but we design deliberately so that a failure in one layer does not expose your data.

14. Your rights

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), and other data protection laws that may apply to you, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and receive a copy
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Ask us to delete your data — for the tournament app you can also do this yourself, in the app; see section 6
  • Withdraw consent you previously gave
  • Object to or ask us to restrict certain processing
  • Ask for your data in a portable format
  • Complain to the relevant data protection authority

To exercise any of these, email info@deltagroup-me.com. We will respond within 30 days. A parent or guardian may exercise these rights for their child.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the GDPR or UK GDPR may also apply to our processing of your data, and you have equivalent rights under those laws.
