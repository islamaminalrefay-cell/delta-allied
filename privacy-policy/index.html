Privacy Policy

Football League - DOFA  ·  Effective 14 February 2026  ·  Updated 28 September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application “Football League - DOFA”, published by Delta Allied Sports.

This page covers the mobile app. Delta Allied Sports also runs tournaments, a registration service and a tournament app through its website — those are covered by our full privacy policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect limited user data to operate the app, including:

  • Basic account information (such as login via Apple or Google)
  • In-app activity (game progress, team data, and usage)
  • Purchase information (processed securely via Apple and Google; we do not store payment details)

2. How we use it

Data is used only to:

  • Provide and improve app functionality
  • Enable gameplay features
  • Process in-app purchases

3. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The app is designed for general audiences, and any features involving purchases or external links are protected by a parental gate.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple and Google through their app stores. They handle the payment and hold their own records of it; we never receive or store your card details.

5. Data storage and security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

Your data may be stored on secure servers within or outside the UAE, in compliance with applicable regulations.

6. Data retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

7. Your rights

Depending on applicable UAE laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

To exercise your rights, please contact us using the details below.

8. Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your game account and the data held with it at any time, by emailing Info@deltagroup-me.com.

Our account deletion page sets out exactly what is deleted, what is kept and why, how long it takes, and what happens to purchases.

9. Third-party links

Our platforms may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such external sites.

10. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on our website with the updated effective date.
