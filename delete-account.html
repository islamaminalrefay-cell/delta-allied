Delete your account

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your Delta Allied Sports tournament app account and the personal data held with it. You can do it yourself inside the app, or ask us to do it for you.

Deleting is permanent. We cannot restore an account or its details afterwards. If you only want to stop receiving match notifications, you can turn those off in the app instead — that does not delete anything.

Delete it yourself, in the app

This is the fastest route, and it takes effect immediately.

Option 1 — in the app

From your account settings

  1. Open the Delta Allied Sports tournament app and sign in.
  2. Go to [EXACT IN-APP PATH — e.g. Profile → Settings → Delete account].
  3. Confirm when asked. Your account and personal details are deleted straight away.

Or ask us to delete it

If you cannot sign in, no longer have the app installed, or would simply rather we did it, ask us directly. You do not need to reinstall the app.

Option 2 — by request

Send us a deletion request

Use our contact form, or email Mounir.imam@deltagroup-me.com with the subject Account deletion request.

So we can find the right account and be sure the request is really yours, please include:

  • The email address or phone number your account uses
  • The full name on the account
  • If you are a parent or guardian asking on behalf of a child, say so and give the child's name

We may ask one follow-up question to confirm your identity. We will not ask for a password.

What is kept, and why

One thing does not get erased: the record of matches that have already been played.

If you scored a goal, received a card, refereed a fixture or confirmed a result, that stays in the competition's records — but with nothing linking it to you. Your name is removed, so what remains does not identify you.

We keep it because a finished tournament's results have to stay accurate and complete. Deleting the events from a match that other teams played in would change the standings of a competition that is already over, for everyone in it. Historical squad lists are kept on the same basis, showing an entry with no name attached.

Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

If you organise tournaments

Every tournament has to have an organizer, so if your account currently runs one, it cannot simply be removed — someone has to take it over first.

When you ask to delete an account in this position, you will be told exactly which tournaments need transferring, and deletion continues once you have named the person taking over. We never reassign a tournament to someone without being told to, and we never quietly delete one instead.

The person taking over needs an app account of their own already, since otherwise they would have no way to administer what they were given.

How long it takes

Deleting from inside the app happens immediately. A request sent to us is actioned within [e.g. 30 days], and usually much sooner. Backups are overwritten on our provider's normal cycle.
