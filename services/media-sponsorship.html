Media & Sponsorship

A big team of photographers.

Filming every match and event with proper sports media equipment, running interviews, broadcasting live, and managing the social channels that turn a tournament day into content people actually watch.

What we actually do

A tournament that isn't documented well might as well not have happened, as far as sponsors and parents are concerned. We put a real media team on the ground for every event we run — sports photographers who know how to shoot fast-moving youth football and basketball, not just stand on the sideline with a long lens.

That covers the full chain: match photography and highlight film, player and coach interviews, live broadcast of key matches, and the social media management that gets the content out while the tournament is still happening, not three weeks later.

Why it's run this way

Sponsors don't just want their logo on a banner — they want to see it in content that actually gets watched. Parents want to see their kid's save, not just a final scoreline. And academies want proof of the standard they're competing at. Good media coverage is what makes all three of those things true at once.

Running media in-house, rather than farming it out per event, means the crew already understands the sport, the age groups, and what actually makes a good sports photo — which matters more than it sounds like it should.

What this looks like in practice

  • Full-day match photography across every pitch or court running that day
  • Player, coach, and official interviews for club and sponsor content
  • Live broadcast of finals and marquee matches
  • Social media management before, during, and after the event
  • Sponsor-ready highlight reels and photo packages
